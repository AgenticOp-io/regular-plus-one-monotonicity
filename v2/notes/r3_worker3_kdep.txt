REPLACEMENT TEXT for the "Dependence on K" paragraph of Thm growing-degree-factor (LaTeX-ready)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\emph{Dependence on \(K\).}  Put \(\xi=K-nq/2\).  Treating \(\Phi_0\) as a function of \(\bar a=2K/n\), with the class
sums at their means under the exchangeable law given \(K\) (the exponential tilt of \(h\) with mean \(\bar a\)), one computes
exactly (\texttt{v2/scripts/r3\_worker3\_ell.py}) at \(K=nq/2\)
\[
n\,\partial_K\Phi_0=-\frac{D^3(D-2L)^2}{L^2(D-1)^2(D-2)(D-L)^2},\qquad |n^2\partial_K^2\Phi_0|=O(1),
\]
the \(-\lambda(g)^2\) slope \(\approx-(D-2L)/n\) being cancelled by the other three exponents.  So
\(\ell:=\partial_K\Phi_0=O(1/n)\) and \(\partial_K^2\Phi_0=O(n^{-2})\) uniformly on \(W\), whence
\(\sup_W\lvert\Phi_0(K)-\Phi_0(nq/2)\rvert=O\bigl(C_0D\sigma_K/n+C_0^2D^2\sigma_K^2/n^2\bigr)
=O\bigl(\sqrt{D/n}+L^2D/n\bigr)\to0\), using \(\sigma_K^2=O(nL^2/D)\) and \(D^3=o(n)\).  Therefore
\(\EE_Q[e^{\Phi_0(K)}\mathbf 1_W]=e^{\Phi_0(nq/2)}\,Q(W)\,(1+o(1))\).  No local limit theorem beyond the centre is used:
\(Q(W)\to1\) is the statement that the terms outside \(W\) are negligible, which is the Chernoff bound of the next paragraph
(there \(x_{\mathrm e}/p=O(D^{3/2}/(L\sqrt n))\to0\) is exactly \(D^3=o(L^2n)\), implied by \(D^3=o(n)\)); the inside sum is
\(\sqrt{(D-1)/(D-2)}(1+o(1))(\EE Z)^2\) by the ordinary local limit theorem at \(O(\omega\sigma_K)\) from the centre.
