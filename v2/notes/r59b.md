## Idea
Window W={t<=2/n}={G>=n/4}, G=chi2/2~Gamma(a), a=(n+1)/2 (tail t>2/n is r57(a)). I(s)=E[e^{st};W] is entire in s, no mgf problem. Substituting G=a e^{-y}... (y=ln(a/G)-type variable) gives, with zeta=s/(2a^2),
I(s)=(a^a/Gamma(a)) J(zeta), J=int_{y0}^inf e^{a psi}, psi(y)=y-e^y+zeta e^{-y}, saddle X*=(1+w)/2, w=sqrt(1-4 zeta), and along y=y*+r the exponent is exactly (r-sinh r)-w(cosh r-1).
1. J(zeta)=main(1+E), main=sqrt(2pi/(a w))e^{a psi*}; steepest-contour/tail estimate gives |E|<=eps(a) for |zeta|<=Z0=0.02, n>=20, explicit constants (c=0.709, Delta=0.091 in script); eps(a)<=0.35 at a=10, 0.017 at a=1000, <=1/2 for all tabulated a (monotonicity in a between table points not proved; it is a sum of decreasing terms in the formula).
2. Hence I(s)!=0 on |s|<=R=2a^2 Z0 (zero-free disc, radius ~0.04 a^2 ~ 0.01 n^2).
3. ln I(s)/I(0) is analytic there, Re <= M:=4R/n (from a psi*(zeta)-a psi*(0), first term q1 s). Borel-Caratheodory on |s|<=R/2, then Cauchy: |kappa_j^W|<= j! M (2/R)^j for ALL j, and remainder after order K: |ln I - T_K| <= M x^{K+1}/(1-x), x=|s|/(R/2) ~ 50 L/n for s=nL/2.
4. K=2 gives remainder ~ 10^4 L^3/n^2 for all |s|<=nL/2 with L<=n/50 -- the true size of kappa3 s^3/6 = L^3/(3n^2). This replaces "4th order, L=o(n^{5/12})" in the pure case; no L=o(sqrt n) restriction arises from this step.
