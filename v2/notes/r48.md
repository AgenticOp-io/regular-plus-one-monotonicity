# r48: gap 2, k=3 cluster contributions -- they do NOT cap the range at n^{2/5}; all pieces are O(L^4/n^2) (numerics, mean-field)

Scripts (all run): `v2/scripts/r48_k3_check.py` (brute-force validation), `r48_gamma3.py` (symbolic gamma3 + backward-recursion shift),
`r48_direct3.py` (direct k=3 term summed over j). Setup = r46: regular F (deg L), regular U_j (deg j-1), uniform overlap o=2m/n, x=2,
tree-like local structure (triangles dropped: they cost O(L^3/n^3) per factor), m_j fixed at its mean jL/2, mpmath 60 digits.

## 1. Degree-3 cluster formula (PROVED elementary + CHECKED)
ln Z (degree 3 in w) = r36's degree-2 part + q3 e3 - q1 q2 e1 e2 + q1^3 e1^3/3, with e3 the 3-matching sum
e3 = D3/6 - (A1 - B + Tr) (D3 = e1^3-3e1Q2+2Q3; A1 = adjacent-pair x third edge; B = "cherries" of adjacency; Tr = mutually adjacent triples),
by 1[p>=1] = p - C(p,2) + C(p,3). Brute-force check on a random n=16 graph: e2, e3 match enumeration to 1e-9;
truncation error of exact ln Z falls from 1.5e-3 -> 2.7e-4 (a=.5) and 6.3e-3 -> 2.2e-4 (a=1) on adding degree 3 (n=16, not asymptotic; only validates the formula).

## 2. gamma3 = d/dm of the degree-3 term (NUMERICS + sympy series)
gamma3 n^3/L^2 (j=L/2, y=1.5) = 2.9e-2 (L=10), 1.2e-2 (30), 3.7e-3 (100), 1.2e-3 (300), the same at n=1e4,1e6,1e8: gamma3 scales like n^{-3}, NOT n^{-2}. (r36's "O(L^3/n^2) per factor" is the size of ln Z_3 itself, not of its m-slope; the slope loses a factor ~L/n
because the leading n^{-2} pieces of ln Z_3 are m-independent: m enters only via W1' = -a and o=2m/n.)
Backward-recursion shift with gamma2+gamma3 vs gamma2: dS3 = -0.031 L^5/n^3 (n=1e6,1e8, L=100,300; dS3 n^2/L^5 = -3.1e-8 at n=1e6, -3.1e-10 at 1e8).
Compare k<=2: 0.083 L^4/n^2 (reproduced: 0.0825-0.0833). Ratio dS3/dS2 ~ 0.4 L/n.  => o(1) for L=o(n^{3/5}); no constraint beyond n^{1/2}.

## 3. Direct k=3 term  S3 = sum_j [lnZ3_j(y_j;m=0) - lnZ3_j(1;m=0)]   (NUMERICS)
S3 n^2/L^4 = 0.0375, 0.067, 0.078, 0.0816 (L=10,30,100,300), same at n=1e6 and 1e8 -> S3 -> L^4/(12 n^2) (guess of the constant 1/12).
So the deterministic k=3 contribution to ln E[x^T] is O(L^4/n^2) = o(1) for L=o(n^{1/2}). Not L^5/n^2.

## 4. Verdict (labels)
- k=3, mean-field/tree-like, m at mean, x=2: total shift O(L^5/n^3) (backward map) and O(L^4/n^2) (direct); both o(1) for L=o(n^{1/2}). **NUMERICS/SKETCH**. The n^{2/5} cap suggested in r46 s4 is **FALSE at this level** (the feared O(L^2/n^2) per-m term is absent, it is O(L^2/n^3)).
- Not shown / OPEN: (i) rigorous KP bound for signed clusters k>=3 (gap 2 as a theorem); k>=4 was not computed (pattern suggests L^{k+1}/n^{k-1}... for direct, unverified);
  (ii) fluctuations of m_j around the mean and of o_v (non-uniform overlap) -- only the mean profile used; (iii) triangles / short cycles dropped; (iv) x=2 only;
  (v) gaps 3, 4 conditional as before; the S proxy sum_j j(y_j-1) is r45's assumption, but dS3 is so small that the proxy is irrelevant.
So gap 2 at k=3 does not obstruct L=o(n^{1/2}); the range question is now fully back to rigour (signed-cluster convergence + overlap tails), not to size of k=3.
