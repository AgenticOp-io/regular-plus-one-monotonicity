METHOD.  Exact P(uv in G | H1 in G) for uniform D-regular G on n vertices.  N(d) = #labelled graphs of degree sequence d via a
DP on the class counts (c_1..c_D) (checked: N(3^6)=70, N(3^8)=19355, N(3^10)=11180820, and vs brute force on irregular
sequences).  #regular graphs containing S = f(D-deg_S, S), f(d,S) = #graphs of degree d avoiding S, and the recursion
f(d,S) = sum_{T<=S} (-1)^|T| f(d-deg_T, T) (graphs of degree d containing T = graphs of degree d-deg_T avoiding T;
the T=S term has strictly smaller d, so it terminates).  Check: P(edge)=D/(n-1) exactly for n=6,10,14.
Gao's principal term is transcribed exactly as in r2_worker3 Hypothesis T1 (A with the W-sum, B=phibar/Mt, H2 empty).

ALGEBRA (rigorous, trivial).  e^{-A-B} - (1-A)(1-B) = (A^2+B^2)/2 + O(|A|^3+|B|^3).  Hence, given Thm 6 with its
unspecified eta, the exp form is EQUIVALENT to  eta = -(A^2+B^2)/2 + O((1+Delta_H1^2)/n^2 + D^3/n^3), i.e. to knowing the
second-order switching term of Gao to that precision.  Since A^2, B^2 ~ D^2/n^2 = size(eta), nothing below the second-order
term of Gao's expansion can decide it; an 'order-independence' argument alone does not either: it only forces the curl of
eta (as a function of the state H1 along two orders of adding edges) to vanish, a linear constraint on eta that the product
form and the exp form both satisfy to the order tested (they differ by a gradient-like term in the exact star/empty cases),
so it cannot select the exp form.  A proof therefore needs a new second-order computation (McKay-type switching with
forbidden edges to relative error O(1/n^2)); I did not do it.
