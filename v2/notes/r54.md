# r54: pairing-level check of the sharp rate and the band m ~ delta/2

Choice: sharp-rate item, not config-split (iii). Reason: config-split needs a new selection argument
beyond single orbits and I have no concrete next step; the sharp rate has an exactly computable
pairing-level half that can be tested, so I tested it. Nothing here is a proof for simple graphs.

## What was run
`scripts/r54_pairing_relvar.py` (exact 1-D formula of Lemma factor-one-dim in float64/lgamma; checked
equal to the exact Fraction formula in `fixed_degree_factor/exact_one_dim.py` to ~1e-14 at
(4,3,1),(6,4,2),(10,6,3)). `scripts/r54_band.py`: d=6,12,24,40, many m including d/2-1, d/2, d/2+1,
n=400 and 1600. Re-run both; output above is what I got.

## Findings
1. **(numerics, exact formula, not a proof)** The raw PAIRING relvar of the m-factor count equals
   (1-x)^(-1/2)-1, x=1/(d-1) (loops+double edges, k>=1 cycles, sum x^k/(2k) = -1/2 ln(1-x)),
   not 1/(6 d^3). Relative error <=1e-3 at n=1600 for every tested m. This is the
   Lemma factor-config value sqrt((d-1)/(d-2)); consistent with STATUS. The 1/(6d^3) lives only
   after conditioning on simplicity (k>=3 cycles). Do not compare the raw pairing number to 1/(6d^3).
2. **(numerics)** Band m~d/2: no anomaly. m=d/2-1,d/2,d/2+1 agree with the m-free limit; symmetric under
   m<->d-m as stated. So at pairing level the band is not an obstruction; any band problem is
   in the simple-graph transfer (McKay weights), as the Remark says.
3. **(numerics)** n-correction: n*(relvar - lim) is flat in n (400 vs 1600), -0.012 (d=40, m=d/2),
   -0.019 (d=24), -0.031 (d=12): ~ -0.5/d, i.e. O(1/(d n)). For m=1 it is positive, +0.014 (d=40), ~0.56/d.
   So there is NO d^2 m/n term at pairing level (coef/d^2 -> 0); the conjecture's d^2 m/n term
   is purely an artefact of the simple-graph transfer, if present at all.
