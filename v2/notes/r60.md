## Chain (each step with its constant)
Write Z_W=E[e^{Phi};W].
**Step A (window vs full) -- PROVED from H3.** Z_W<=Z, Z-Z_W=E[e^Phi;t>2/n]<=T. So 0<= ln Z - ln Z_W <= -ln(1-eps) <= 2 e^{-0.02n}. (Note: E e^{st} over all t diverges for s>0, so "full cumulant generating function" does not exist; only moment cumulants kappa_j(t) do. Everything below uses the windowed mgf I(s)=E[e^{st};W], as r59b.)
**Step B (linearisation) -- PROVED from H1.** Taylor with Lagrange remainder: Phi(q1+tau)=Phi(q1)+a tau+R, |R|<=(A0/2)tau^2 on W. (No Phi''' needed; Phi'' is the deterministic number Phi''(q1) up to the Lagrange point.) Hence Z_W=e^{Phi(q1)-a q1} I_t... precisely ln Z_W = Phi(q1) + ln E[e^{a tau};W] + ln E_a[e^R], E_a the tilted law on W with density prop. to e^{a tau}.
**Step C (the R-term, the "typical size" argument, now proved).** Tilted log-density of t: l(t)=-(a_G+1)ln t-1/(2t)+a t, l''=t^{-2}(a_G+1-1/t). On C=(0,4/(3n+6)]: l''<=-(n/4)t^{-2}<=-kappa, kappa=n^3/8 (n>=20): strongly concave. Mode t*: t*-q1=delta~|a|/|l''|<=c1 nL*2/n^3 ~ 2c1 L/n^2 <= 1/(100n) for L<=n/200 c1<=1/2, so t* is interior to C. Local lower bound for the normaliser (|l''|<=3n^3 near t*) gives P_a(|t-t*|>r; C)<= C' exp(-n^3 r^2/16) (C' absolute). Then
 E_a[e^{R};C] <= e^{A0 delta^2} E_a[e^{A0 (t-t*)^2};C] <= e^{A0 delta^2} (1-16 A0/n^3)^{-1/2} (1+small),
 so 0<=... |ln E_a e^R| <= C''(A0/n^3 + A0 delta^2)= O(c0 L^2/n^2 + c0 c1^2 L^4/n^3) <= C''' L^3/n^2 (L<=n).
 Outside C (t in (4/(3n+6),2/n]): P_a <= e^{-0.02n}-type by the same Chernoff as r57(a), while e^{R}<=e^{A0*4/n^2}=e^{4c0 L^2/n}<=e^{c0 n/10^4}: negligible iff c0/10^4 < 0.02 -- an assumption on c0 (open: c0 not explicit). Lower bound ln E_a e^R>=E_a R>=-A0 E_a tau^2/2 gives the other side from the same estimate.
Numerics (A0=nL^2, a=nL/2, n=200..3200, L up to n/200): ln E_a e^R / (L^2/n^2) = 1.08,1.63(L=10,n=200),1.02,1.13,1.005,1.03,1.06: the predicted A0*kappa2/2=L^2/n^2 leading term, L^3/n^2 not even needed. (Statistical check of the lemma, not a proof; quadrature on t in (1e-3/n,2/n].)
**Step D (pure case) -- r59b + window cumulants.** I(s)=P(W)exp(sum kappa_j^W s^j/j!). Cauchy/BC (H2) with K=2: |ln I(a)/P(W) - kappa_1^W a - kappa_2^W a^2/2| <= M x^3/(1-x)=: B_L, B_L<=C_B L^3/n^2 for x<=1/2 (C_B~ 0.04 n*8 x^3 n^... ~ 3e5 (200c1)^3/ 8e6... = large, unoptimised, 1e5x loose per r59b numerics).
**Step E (window cumulants vs moment cumulants kappa_j(t)) -- PROVED for j=1,2 (all we use).** m_k^W=E[t^k;W]/P(W), E[t^k;W^c]=q_k P_k, P_k<=e^{-0.0271n} (k<=n/8, r57), P_0<=e^{-0.0966n}. |m_k^W-q_k|<=q_k (P_k+P_0)/(1-P_0)<=2q_k e^{-0.027n}.
