DERIVATION (pairing model + conditioning on simplicity; does NOT use the n^-1 cancellation)
1. Pairing model, exact: E_pair[T] = (n)_3/6 (D(D-1))^3 / ((M-1)(M-3)(M-5)), M=nD
   = (D-1)^3/6 (1 + (-3 + 9/D)/n + ...).
2. E[T | simple] = E_pair[T] * P(simple | triangle on fixed abc)/P(simple). Given the triangle, the rest is a pairing on
   M-6 stubs with three vertices of degree D-2. With Lambda = E[loops]+E[double edges] (loops S/2M, doubles S^2/4M^2, S=sum d(d-1)):
   change = loops (-3D+6)/(nD) + doubles -3(D-1)(D-2)/(nD) + extra parallel edges to triangle edges 3(D-2)^2/(nD) = -6(D-2)/(nD).
   So the ratio is 1 + 6(D-2)/(nD).
3. r1 = (-3+9/D) + 6(D-2)/D = 3(D-1)/D.  sympy confirms.
