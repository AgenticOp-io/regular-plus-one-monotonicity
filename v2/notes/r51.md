## Tested (numerics, exact integer N_j, n<=10, cycle/path hosts only)
Is N_j = #{simple D-regular G : |E(G) cap H| = j} log-concave (N_j^2 >= N_{j-1}N_{j+1})?
- D=3,4,5 at n=8,9,10 (5 cases, all 12-16 hosts each): **yes, every host**.
- D=2 (n=9,10) and D=n-3 (n=7 D=4, n=9 D=6): **no** (fails on C_n and long paths) -- same degenerate cases where r49 refuted Re z<=0.
- Ultra-log-concavity vs Binomial(k,.) normalisation: **fails** nearly everywhere (wrong comparison; N_j is not binomial-like). Do not retry.
- n=11 D=4 enumeration with r49_enum.c did not finish in ~10 min (killed); no n>=11 data.

## What this does and does not give
- Log-concavity of positive N_j is NOT zero-freeness (only real-rootedness implies it, and r47/r49 show complex roots for C_5, C_6, ...). So it does not feed Barvinok. **Not a route to the bound.**
- Pattern across r47/r49/r51: every structural property (Re z<0, log-concavity) holds on D in [3, n-4] and fails exactly at D=2 and D=n-3, so any restricted-range statement must exclude those. Consistent with, but no evidence for, uniformity in n (n<=10, margin for D=5 is only 0.18, not growing).
