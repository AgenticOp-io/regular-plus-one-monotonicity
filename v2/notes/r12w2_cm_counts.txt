SIMPLE-GRAPH CONDITIONING (proof sketch, standard):
 For fixed small simple H (e,v edges/vertices; excess e-v>=0), E_CM[N_H] = n^{v-e}*c_H*(1+O(1/n)) exactly given by falling factorials.
 Condition on H embedded: remaining stubs are a uniform pairing of nD-2e stubs with degrees D except O(1) vertices lowered. By
 Bender-Canfield / Bollobas / McKay-Wormald (Janson 2009 "The probability that a random multigraph is simple" for degree seqs with
 sum d^2 = O(n)): P(rest simple and no parallel to H) = exp(-(D^2-1)/4)(1+O(1/n)) because the exponent lambda=(M2/2M)+(M2/2M)^2 depends smoothly on the O(1) degree changes (relative O(1/n)) and the extra O(1) forbidden pairs each cost O(1/n).
 Hence E_simple[N_H] = E_CM[N_H](1+O(1/n)). For diamonds (E=O(1/n)) the error is O(n^-2), i.e. relative n^-3 per directed edge: irrelevant for c0.
 For C4, eL, pairs likewise (they appear multiplied by n^-1 prefactors). For TRIANGLES the 1/n correction matters (r1): E_simple[T]=(D-1)^3/6 (1+r1/n) with
 r1=3(D-1)/D NOT derived here (conditioning gives O(1/n) shift; value would follow from McKay 2011 "Subgraphs of random graphs with specified degrees" Thm 1.3 or Gao-Wormald; not checked).
 Uniform-simple vs CM-conditioned: identical (simple graphs have equal pairing multiplicity D!^n), so no extra weight.
