# r47 — new routes for (R2*) past n^{1/3} and L1' (honest report)

Scope: only this note and `v2/scripts/r47_*.py`. Nothing proved; no gap closed.
Labels: proved / sketch / numerics / false.

## Routes triaged (no computation = reasoning only)

- (a) Stein / Chen-Stein for cycle counts. **Sketch, not pursued.** In the pairing model
  the cycle counts are already near-Poisson with exact factorial moments. The hard part
  is conditioning on simplicity, and Chen-Stein bounds total variation of counts. It does
  not control a *ratio* P(simple | host)/P(simple), which is what L1' needs. It is the same
  missing input as the Bonferroni route.
- (e) Janson size-bias couplings. Same obstruction. The coupling error is the local
  variation of ln P(simple), i.e. L1' itself.
- (c) Bethe permanent / loop calculus. **Sketch, not tested.** The exact identity
  is Z = Z_Bethe * sum over generalized loops. For D-regular simple graphs the Bethe
  term is the McKay main term. The loop sum is the cycle expansion we are trying to
  control, so the identity restates the problem. It is not independent.
- (d) Full-weight Hubbard-Stratonovich. Not done. Gaussian decoupling of e^{k^2/n}
  already failed in r44. Decoupling the full weight introduces phi(k) = k^2/n + psi(k),
  and psi is not Gaussian-decouplable (r44: not product form).

## (b) Lee-Yang / Barvinok zero-free interpolation — tested

Goal: if P_H(z) = sum_{G simple D-regular} z^{|E(G) ∩ H|} (H a host with L edges) were
zero-free in a fixed neighbourhood of [0,1], then ln P_H would be analytic with uniform
error and the L = o(n^{1/3}) barrier would go.

**Test 1 (numerics, `r47_leeyang.py`)**: exact enumeration, n=7,8, D=3,4. Hosts are cycles
C_3..C_8 and paths. Roots of P_H:
- Real-rooted for C_3, C_4 (n=8, D=3 and D=4).
- Complex roots for C_5, C_6, C_8 and paths (max |Im| about 0.2 to 2.9).
- **Every root has Re z <= 0** in all 21 cases (a root at exactly 0 occurs only when N_0=0, n=7 C_5, C_6).
  So the half-plane Re z > 0 is zero-free, which includes [0,1] and the disc |z-1| < 1.
- Nearest root to z=1 was at distance >= 1.04 (n=7, D=4, C_3; every other case farther).
- Caveat: n <= 8 is far too small to extrapolate. Heilmann-Lieb real-rootedness holds for
  D=1 (matchings) but the C_5/C_6 complex roots show it does not hold for D>=2.
  Half-plane stability is not a known theorem for D-regular graphs. The
  generating polynomial prod(1+y_e x_u x_v) is not real stable, so Borcea-Branden does not apply.

**Test 2 (numerics, `r47_polydisc.py`)**: Barvinok's real requirement is a polydisc |y_e - 1| <= rho
on ALL edges (n=8, D=3, 28 edges), found by random hill-climb on the torus |y_e - 1| = rho
(minimising |Z(y)|/Z(1)).
- rho = 0.3 already gives min |Z|/Z(1) = 0.014, i.e. nearly a zero.
  The polydisc radius is therefore at most about 0.3 at n=8, D=3.
- Larger rho (manager re-run, `r47_polydisc.py 8 3`): rho=0.5 min 0.00024, rho=0.7 min 0.0 (a zero on the polydisc).
- Reading: for degree-constrained counts the all-edge polydisc radius shrinks (heuristically
  like 1/D or worse, since each vertex constraint couples D edges and E-fluctuations of
  |G ∩ H| are O(1) per vertex). A host-only tilt (L edges) can be much better, but Test 1 is
  the only evidence, and it is at n <= 8.

**Verdict on (b): FAILS as a proof route, as stated.** Zero-freeness of P_H uniform in n and D is
exactly as hard as L1': it is a statement about the same polynomial whose log-variation we
cannot bound. Barvinok interpolation converts zero-freeness into analytic expansion, but
supplies no zero-freeness. The only known mechanism for D-regular (Lee-Yang / Heilmann-Lieb)
is D=1.

## What this does leave (labelled)

- **numerics**: half-plane zero-freeness of P_H on n <= 8 hosts (21 cases). A conjecture, **not proved**.
- **sketch**: if Re z > 0 zero-freeness held uniformly in n, D (Conjecture HP), then
  ln P_H(z) is analytic on a neighbourhood of [0,1] and Barvinok's lemma gives the cumulant
  expansion with error O(L^j / n^{j-1}) per truncation order j. That would remove the n^{1/3}
  barrier. This is conditional, and Conjecture HP is itself open.
- **false** as a route: Test 2 shows a naive all-edge polydisc (Barvinok's stock hypothesis) is NOT zero-free
  at radius 0.3 for n=8. Stock Barvinok interpolation over all edges cannot give uniform-in-n.

## Suggested next step (not done)

Test Conjecture HP at n=9,10 (D=3, 4, 5) by exact counting through a degree-sequence DP
(count graphs with a given host-intersection profile) instead of enumeration, and check whether
the nearest-root distance to z=1 grows like n/D. If it does, HP is plausible and the proof target
becomes a Hurwitz-type argument via the Newton-type log-concavity of N_j. Log-concavity of
N_j is visible in the n=8 tables but untested at scale.
